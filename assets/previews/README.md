# Preview thumbnails

AI-generated with FLUX.2 klein 4B (Apache-2.0) on this machine: one synthetic adult per look (Man, Woman, Non-binary, Alien, Monster), always fully clothed, one attribute changed per image. They show no real person, brand or logo. Each file, its prompt scaffold, framing and seed are listed in `manifest.json` and can be rebuilt with `scripts/thumbs/`.

Set of 543 shipped thumbnails (all generated items are shipped, none excluded), 2.9 MB in total, each at most 25 KB, lazy-loaded webp. Includes 55 creature-look tiles (fox character) for outfit, accessory and prop, used for the creature types. Covers every option of every builder control, including the silly and creature options, shoes, props, eye shape, head shape, neck and height. Every image was reviewed on contact sheets for adult, fully clothed, SFW and non-demeaning. Anything missing would fall back to drawn art in the app, but nothing is missing.
